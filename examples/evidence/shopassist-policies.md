# ShopAssist policies

Source version: v2.1. Updated 4 September 2026.

Most unused items can be returned within 30 days.

Electronics can be returned within 14 days.

Clearance items are final sale unless they arrive damaged or defective.

Approved refunds return to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.

Electronics include a 12-month manufacturer warranty for defects. Accidental damage is not covered.

Standard shipping usually takes 3–5 business days. The order confirmation contains the specific estimate.
